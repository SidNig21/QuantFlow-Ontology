# Polar (2605.24220) — leverage

**One line:** Reconstruct token-faithful trajectories from arbitrary harnesses — peer-bus + seat PTYs already produce traces; Polar teaches how to make them *trainable*.

## Why it fits

Peer messages land as content-addressed `trajectory` Artifacts (finetune store). Polar's async RL-over-any-harness pattern is how you later train without ripping out Hermes/Electron.

## Best product leverage

1. **Trajectory schema** — Ensure Kernel Artifacts store enough structure (tool calls, peer turns, outcomes) to rebuild token-level traces later.
2. **Harness-agnostic logging** — Same Artifact shape whether seat is Hermes, Codex, or a scraper species.
3. **Phase 6 trainer adapter** — When Evolve starts, adapter reads Kernel trajectories → Polar-like rollout format; Kernel remains truth, trainer is a consumer.

## Do not

- Do not put Polar (or any RL server) on the write path of the Kernel.
- Do not log secrets/API keys into trajectories (hard stop).

## Steal checklist

- [ ] Audit current trajectory Artifact fields vs "token-faithful" needs
- [ ] Document redact rules for trajectories
- [ ] Defer actual Polar/async RL infra until Evaluation history exists

## Why it fits

Phase 0 seats are real PTYs. TMAX targets Terminal-Bench-style skill. QuantFlow's generated tools will be CLI/MCP shaped; seats must be good at that surface.

## Best product leverage

1. **Species finetune (Phase 6)** — When trajectory store is thick, finetune a local model for `worker` seats on QuantFlow tool traces + TMAX-style terminal tasks.

3. **Seat registry metadata** — Tag AgentDefinitions with recipe/version (`tmax-inspired-v1`) so Reports know which species weights ran.

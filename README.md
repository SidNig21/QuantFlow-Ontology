# QuantFlow Ontology

QuantFlow is Ryan's Windows-first personal sports-betting **research and advisory** desk. The Director
coordinates admitted AI participants and useful data, tools, methods, and compute from the Dock. Their
work appears on one Canvas; Inspect shows its evidence and history. The Kernel/Ontology owns the exact
shared record of questions, assignments, sources, calculations, criticism, revisions, and later outcomes.
QuantFlow never chooses a stake or places a bet or trade. Ryan acts outside the app.

## Product boundary

Participants are colleagues with independent private contexts and governed seats. A Data, Tool, Method,
or Compute item supplies a useful capability or surface; it is not a pretend terminal colleague. Both
enter through the Dock and work against the same Kernel truth. The Director is the default coordinator,
while Ryan can also open permitted equipment or address another participant directly. A current market
price is distinct from evidence that a sporting claim is true. A Critic can assess whether work followed
its evidence and method; later outcomes are needed to test predictive usefulness.

The normal Windows app, real provider data, actual participant runtimes, and independent verification
are required for live product claims. Fixtures and documentation preserve regression evidence; they do
not turn an unbuilt capability into working software.

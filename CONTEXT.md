# QuantFlow Ontology

QuantFlow is Ryan's sports research workspace for working with AI participants, data, and analytical equipment. This glossary defines intended product meaning; a definition does not claim that the capability is implemented or accepted.

## Workspace

**Canvas**:
The one spatial workspace where Ryan, participants, and deliberately opened capabilities do visible work. It is never replaced by a Mission, Focus, History, or lineage world.
_Avoid_: Mission view, research world, focus mode, lineage mode

**Director**:
Ryan's primary Hermes participant and the default coordinator of QuantFlow. It is ready on cold open, but it is neither the whole application nor the only participant Ryan may address.
_Avoid_: application, sole front door, orchestrator screen

**Dock**:
The governed catalog of Participants, Data, Tools, Methods, and Compute that Ryan or the Director may bring onto the Canvas. Catalog presence never implies Canvas presence.
_Avoid_: agent launcher, plugin store, second workspace

**Inspect**:
The contextual read-only detail drawer for the selected work, including evidence, calculations, Evaluation, revisions, history, and lineage. It never rearranges or replaces the Canvas.
_Avoid_: Inspect mode, History mode, Full Lineage view

**Cable**:
A temporary visual indication that selected visible surfaces can collaborate or are actively exchanging governed work. No connection means no cable; the Canvas never draws the full Ontology graph.
_Avoid_: ontology edge dump, permanent wiring diagram

**Working surface**:
A deliberately opened place to interact with a participant, capability, or useful result on the Canvas. Its content fits its job while its move, resize, focus, tidy, inspect, and close behavior follows the shared tile grammar.
_Avoid_: database card, separate world, terminal for everything

**Retrieval**:
Deliberately bringing previously recorded work back into the current investigation or onto the Canvas. Retrieved history preserves its original context and does not automatically restart participants or become a current conclusion.
_Avoid_: automatic restoration, resurrected session, new evidence

## Institution

**Inquiry**:
The research request Ryan gives the Director or another participant. The Director normally turns it into a Mission without requiring Ryan to enter a form or another workspace.
_Avoid_: mandatory Technique form, Mission screen

**Mission**:
The internal durable scope that keeps one inquiry and its institutional work together in the Kernel. It is not a Canvas location, tile, mode, or product world.
_Avoid_: workspace, screen, view

**Kernel / Ontology**:
The shared model and authoritative record connecting questions, assignments, evidence, calculations, outputs, criticism, revisions, and outcomes. It preserves what was observed, claimed, done, and reviewed without treating every recorded claim as a fact about the world.
_Avoid_: Canvas database, graph spectacle, shared chat

**Participant**:
A governed runtime seat with a role, independent private context, authorized capabilities, exact Tasks, and attributable outputs. Hermes, Claude Code, and Codex become Participants only after admission through the same contract.
_Avoid_: terminal, model logo, chatbot window

**Role**:
The responsibility a participant has in QuantFlow, such as directing research, investigating evidence, or criticizing a result. A runtime or model brand does not determine that responsibility or its permissions.
_Avoid_: runtime species, provider, model name

**Critic**:
A participant assigned to independently examine exact work for material weaknesses. Its judgment concerns that version and its stated criteria; it does not certify that a sporting prediction is true.
_Avoid_: agreeing agent, automatic approval, truth oracle

**Runtime species**:
The implementation behind a Participant, such as Hermes, Claude Code, or Codex. Species is provenance, never institutional role or authority.
_Avoid_: role, agent identity

**Private context**:
A Participant's own conversation, reasoning, and temporary scratch state. It remains separate from other Participants and is not institutional truth.
_Avoid_: shared memory, evidence

**Shared institutional context**:
The relevant recorded question, assignments, evidence, contributions, decisions, and unresolved issues participants receive for their work. It can grow through explicit exchanges while each participant's private context stays separate.
_Avoid_: group chat, copied transcript, common hidden reasoning

**Collaboration**:
Participants working toward a shared inquiry through assignments, questions, findings, criticism, and revisions that affect each other's work. It includes usable communication and coordination as well as a durable record of consequential exchanges.
_Avoid_: shared storage, terminal collection, automatic agreement

**Contribution**:
An attributable piece of shared work, such as a clarification, finding, proposed change, or partial result. Its status distinguishes a question or proposal from accepted direction, observed evidence, or a reviewed conclusion.
_Avoid_: hidden reasoning, automatically verified fact

**Notification**:
A signal that a participant has new work or a contribution to read. Delivery alone does not mean the participant has understood, accepted, or completed that work.
_Avoid_: assignment, acknowledgment, completed handoff

**Handoff**:
The transfer of exact recorded work into another participant's assignment. The recipient receives the relevant result and context, and responsibility remains identifiable throughout the transfer.
_Avoid_: copied chat, implied ownership, shared context window

**Steering**:
An explicit intervention that clarifies, redirects, stops, or changes responsibility for work. Consequential direction remains attributable and visible to the people and participants affected by it.
_Avoid_: silent prompt change, erased history

**Task**:
One exact durable assignment owned by one Participant at a time. The Director delegates by default; an authorized Participant may create a downstream Task while the Director remains able to observe and steer it.
_Avoid_: prompt, terminal message

**Dependency**:
Recorded work that another Task needs before it can proceed or finish. The dependency names the relevant version and its readiness instead of assuming that a nearby participant has completed it.
_Avoid_: cable, assumed ordering, shared transcript

**Artifact**:
An immutable, attributable work product that Participants can hand off by exact identity. Terminal prose or an unrecorded file is not an institutional handoff.
_Avoid_: chat answer, clipboard handoff

**Run**:
One recorded execution of a calculation, analysis, simulation, or training job with its inputs, method, and outcome. Repeating or materially changing the work remains distinguishable from the original execution.
_Avoid_: entire inquiry, participant conversation, unrecorded calculation

**Evaluation**:
An independent judgment on exact work. Its useful summary may accompany the visible result; its full evidence, limitations, history, and lineage belong in Inspect.
_Avoid_: agreement, agent debate, automatic endorsement

**Revision**:
A new attributable version created when shared work changes in a way that affects its meaning or conclusion. The prior version remains historical, and an earlier Evaluation does not automatically approve the revision.
_Avoid_: silent overwrite, recycled approval, refreshed timestamp

**Report**:
A published research Artifact whose exact work has the required supporting independent Evaluation. A Report can communicate uncertainty or a reason to abstain without claiming that the underlying prediction is correct.
_Avoid_: every agent reply, unreviewed draft, separate inquiry

**Lineage**:
The recorded relationships explaining where work came from, who produced it, what it used, and how it was reviewed or revised. Lineage establishes attribution and history, not the correctness of every claim it contains.
_Avoid_: decorative graph, proof of truth, full transcript

**Stop participant**:
End a participant's current execution while retaining its recorded work. Stopping a participant does not silently complete, cancel, or transfer its unfinished assignments.
_Avoid_: delete research, cancel inquiry, hide tile

**Cancel Task**:
Withdraw a particular assignment with an attributable reason. Cancellation preserves completed evidence and history while preventing an obsolete result from completing that assignment.
_Avoid_: erase work, close surface, terminate every participant

## Equipment and evidence

**Capability**:
Governed Data, Tool, Method, or Compute a Participant may use. A capability never impersonates a Participant.
_Avoid_: agent, seat

**Data**:
A source or collection of material available for research, including market observations, records, documents, transcripts, and media. Its permitted use, coverage, timing, and limitations determine what it can contribute to an inquiry.
_Avoid_: verified truth by default, unrestricted corpus

**Tool**:
Equipment that performs a defined research job over specified inputs and produces an inspectable output. Its availability and allowed use are separate from whether the required software is installed.
_Avoid_: participant, library listing, model logo

**Method**:
A stated procedure for interpreting evidence or carrying out analysis, with assumptions and limits. A method can be used experimentally before it has earned the standing of a reusable Technique.
_Avoid_: agent confidence, unexplained score, mandatory Technique

**Technique**:
A reusable research method whose version and evaluated history justify retaining it for a stated job. Promotion preserves its limits and does not make it reliable for every sport, market, or situation.
_Avoid_: any prompt, one successful pick, untested recipe

**Compute**:
The processing capacity available to carry out research jobs, including local or remote execution. Capacity, cost, availability, and ownership matter when they constrain the work.
_Avoid_: participant, model, unlimited background execution

**Capability readiness**:
Whether equipment is available, configured, permitted, and usable for a particular job now. Being installed or listed in the Dock alone does not establish readiness.
_Avoid_: installed, advertised, always available

**Observation**:
A recorded statement of what a particular source showed at a particular time. An observation can be incomplete, disputed, or later corrected without losing its original attribution.
_Avoid_: timeless fact, inference, final outcome

**Evidence**:
Observations or source material used to support or challenge a particular claim. Its relevance, quality, coverage, and timing remain part of the claim's assessment.
_Avoid_: model assertion, synthetic training example, certainty

**Dataset**:
A defined version of collected material with a stated purpose, coverage, and time boundary. Evidence, context, training, and evaluation uses remain distinguishable so later knowledge cannot silently enter an earlier assessment.
_Avoid_: whatever data is latest, undifferentiated memory, entire database

**Interpretation**:
A participant's explanation of what evidence may mean for an inquiry. It remains distinguishable from source observations and reproducible calculations.
_Avoid_: observation, measured probability, accepted fact

**Calculation**:
A defined numerical transformation of specified inputs under stated rules. Its formula, missing-data behavior, units, and limits remain inspectable wherever its output affects a conclusion.
_Avoid_: prose arithmetic, unexplained number, inferred confidence

**Point-in-time boundary**:
The information cutoff governing a research judgment or historical evaluation. Material unavailable at that cutoff cannot be presented as knowledge the original judgment could have used.
_Avoid_: source date alone, current data applied to the past

**Coverage**:
The population, periods, fields, and market selections actually represented by evidence. Missing coverage remains explicit rather than being treated as a zero value or a complete sample.
_Avoid_: completeness by assumption, empty means zero

**Provenance**:
The source, producer, timing, and method information needed to understand an input or result. Provenance makes work attributable without automatically making it reliable.
_Avoid_: endorsement, correctness certificate

## Betting research

**Hypothesis**:
A specific claim that research can support, challenge, or leave unresolved. Its assessment names the evidence and conditions that would count against it.
_Avoid_: recommendation, guaranteed outcome, vague confidence

**Market event**:
The sporting event to which offered markets refer. A change in schedule or event identity affects freshness and eligibility without erasing earlier observations.
_Avoid_: selection, quote, inquiry

**Market expression**:
A particular offered way to express a research view, such as a fighter winning or a fight ending within a stated round total. Similar wording does not make two selections interchangeable.
_Avoid_: research claim, assumed prop, approximate substitute

**Quote**:
An observation of an offered selection's price and conditions at a particular time. A Quote remains historical evidence when a newer price, suspension, or event change makes it unsuitable for a current judgment.
_Avoid_: permanent price, current price by default, true probability

**Research assessment**:
The evidence-based judgment of whether an inquiry is supported, challenged, or inconclusive. It may exist before Bovada offers a corresponding market.
_Avoid_: bet recommendation, price verdict

**Market actionability**:
The separate judgment of whether a current Bovada selection and price make a researched idea actionable now. It cannot exist without an available, current market expression.
_Avoid_: matchup analysis, unsupported edge claim

**CANDIDATE / WATCH / PASS**:
The three market-actionability outcomes: a currently offered selection worth consideration under stated conditions, an exact condition awaiting change or refresh, or an evidence-backed reason not to act.
_Avoid_: guaranteed bet, pick, wager instruction

**Decision Set**:
The current governed comparison and conclusion for a particular investigation and market observation. It keeps research assessment, market availability, price conditions, and review status distinguishable.
_Avoid_: betting ticket, automatic execution, every historical answer

**Predictive baseline**:
A simple explicit model against which a more elaborate prediction can be compared. Its probabilities require evaluation on cases it did not learn from, using the information available at the relevant time.
_Avoid_: bookmaker price alone, language-model confidence, validated edge

**Scenario simulation**:
Repeated evaluation of possible outcomes under stated assumptions and a defined model. More simulated outcomes reduce sampling noise but do not establish that the assumptions or model are correct.
_Avoid_: proof by volume, prediction without assumptions

**Combination research**:
Analysis of several selections together, including parlays, their dependence, eligibility, and combined price. Separate plausible selections do not establish a defensible joint probability or authorize wager placement.
_Avoid_: independent probabilities by default, ticket execution, guaranteed multiplier

**Outcome**:
The verified result or settlement of an event or selection, including applicable voids and corrections. It enters later evaluation without rewriting what the earlier research knew.
_Avoid_: model prediction, observed price, missing means loss

**Closing price**:
A price observation meeting a declared rule near market close. An unavailable closing observation remains explicitly unavailable rather than being replaced with an arbitrary earlier Quote.
_Avoid_: last convenient price, assumed settlement price

**Closing-line value (CLV)**:
A comparison of an earlier researched price with a qualifying closing price under a stated definition. It is one measure of research performance, not a guarantee of profit or a substitute for outcome and calibration analysis.
_Avoid_: realized profit, universal edge score

**Calibration**:
The relationship between predicted probabilities and observed frequencies across suitable evaluated cases. One correct prediction or one confident participant does not establish calibration.
_Avoid_: hit rate, confidence, agreement

## Continuation and improvement

**Operator Season**:
Repeated use of QuantFlow on real upcoming events to measure usefulness, reliability, and research performance. Missing data, failures, abstentions, and unsuccessful methods remain part of that history.
_Avoid_: successful demo, profit proof, selected winning examples

**Recall**:
Finding relevant earlier work to inform a current inquiry. Recalled material must still be checked for its identity, time boundary, limitations, and current or historical standing.
_Avoid_: new evidence, automatic endorsement, shared private memory

**Specialist model**:
A model adapted for a defined research job and evaluated against a simpler alternative. It supplies reasoning or inference within an admitted participant or capability rather than gaining authority from its training alone.
_Avoid_: automatic expert, separate institution, participant by default

**Training**:
Work that changes a model or policy using designated examples and an explicit objective. Training material and feedback remain distinguishable from independent evaluation and research evidence.
_Avoid_: retrieval, proof of improvement, synthetic sporting truth

**Research policy**:
A versioned rule for choosing research actions, such as which evidence to request next under a budget. A learned policy is evaluated for its defined research job and never gains permission to place wagers or trades.
_Avoid_: trading executor, unrestricted agent autonomy, model confidence

**Method promotion**:
Retaining a method or policy for broader reuse after suitable repeated evaluation. Promotion states the jobs and limits justified by evidence and remains reversible when later results warrant it.
_Avoid_: one-pass success, self-approval, universal certification

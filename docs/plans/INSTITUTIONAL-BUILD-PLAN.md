## Destination

QuantFlow is a founder-operated Windows research institution. Ryan gives one
Research Director a bounded market Mission. The Director uses the same governed
Dock Ryan can use, turns the Mission into durable work, grounds it in named
Technique and point-in-time evidence, recruits heterogeneous specialists,
routes exact evidence through independent review, and returns a visible
Decision Set or an explicit no-candidate result. The Kernel owns the world;
the Canvas makes its current workflow understandable; QuantFlow never places a
bet or trade.

Learning is downstream of trustworthy work. Evaluated history may be recalled,
PufferLib may train market policies, traces may propose institutional changes,
and an owned model may eventually be trained and served. None may write truth,
approve itself, or promote itself.

## Accepted floor

The inherited floor is R0-R17 plus the accepted Pre-R18 coherence correction.
It includes the Windows application, one Hermes Research Director, durable
Mission and Task work, independent evaluation-gated publication, a visible
research world, named Technique selection, operator-supplied settled outcomes,
close/reopen recovery, and a consumer-oriented Canvas projection.

Accepted history is not reopened merely to earn the next rung. Each new rung
proves its new founder-visible capability and the hard seams it changes. A
release door is run only when the active order names it.

not implementation authority.

## Rung contracts

Every rung closes only when all seven verdicts are explicit: **PRODUCT**,
**EVIDENCE**, **ONTOLOGY**, **ATLAS**, **CONSUMER**, **OPERATIONS**, and
**FOUNDER**. `PASS`, `FAIL`, or `NOT APPLICABLE` is allowed; silence is not.

### R18 - Ground & First Use

- **User Value:** Ryan can run one guided NFL Pressure Cascade Mission without
  reading logs or supplying fixture data.
- **Ontology:** the exact Strategy, raw source Artifacts, point-in-time Dataset,
  Run, result Artifact, independent Evaluation, and Report/Decision Set are
  Kernel-held and visibly linked.
- **Operational:** source and credential failures refuse before durable research
  mutation; close/reopen preserves the Mission; shutdown owns its processes.
- **Verdicts:** PRODUCT proves the guided journey; EVIDENCE proves source times,
  raw hashes, and no future data; ONTOLOGY proves exact lineage; ATLAS proves no
  new hard red; CONSUMER proves an unbriefed operator understands the result;
  OPERATIONS proves refusal/reopen/cleanup; FOUNDER decides whether the first
  Mission is useful.

### R19 - Compose

- **User Value:** Ryan and the Director can assemble a useful team containing
  real Hermes and real Claude participants from one Dock.
- **Ontology:** AgentDefinition, AgentSession, terminal tile, Task ownership,
  Artifact, and Evaluation refer to one participant identity; runtime
  replacement does not replace durable work.
- **Operational:** adapter readiness, authentication refusal, cancellation,
  cleanup, and reopen are bounded per runtime.
- **Verdicts:** PRODUCT requires one cross-runtime Artifact-to-Evaluation
  handoff; EVIDENCE binds exact outputs; ONTOLOGY proves identity/lineage;
  ATLAS checks changed launch and write paths; CONSUMER proves recruitment and
  ownership are understandable; OPERATIONS proves replacement and cleanup;
  FOUNDER accepts the mixed team.

### R20 - Supervise & Strategy Lab

- **User Value:** Ryan can change the course of live work, and the Director can
  compare bounded Technique branches, without restarting the desk.
- **Ontology:** steering, refusal, branch ancestry, assignments, second opinions,
  and chosen/pruned candidates are durable actions and links, not chat state.
- **Operational:** invalid or conflicting controls refuse atomically; interrupted
  branches recover without duplicate work.
- **Verdicts:** PRODUCT exercises all founder controls and one bounded branch;
  EVIDENCE preserves each alternative; ONTOLOGY proves transitions and ancestry;
  ATLAS checks touched control/write paths; CONSUMER proves control placement;
  OPERATIONS proves conflict/reopen; FOUNDER accepts controllability.

### R21 - Remember

- **User Value:** Ryan sees relevant evaluated prior work and why it matched.
- **Ontology:** one immutable recall receipt cites exact prior Strategy, Dataset,
  Run, Artifact, Evaluation, and Report lineage; the new Mission remains current.
- **Operational:** deterministic ordering, bounded retrieval, no-match behavior,
  and reopen are explicit.
- **Verdicts:** PRODUCT exposes evaluated recall; EVIDENCE shows provenance and
  current-time revalidation; ONTOLOGY proves recall cannot authorize truth;
  ATLAS checks retrieval/write boundaries; CONSUMER distinguishes context from
  current evidence; OPERATIONS proves bounded/reopen behavior; FOUNDER accepts
  usefulness. The preserved former R18 recall draft is input, not authority.

### R22 - Learn the Market

- **User Value:** Ryan can compare a learned market policy against the named
  baseline and decide whether it deserves promotion.
- **Ontology:** Environment, training Dataset, Run, Policy Artifact, held-out
  Evaluation, promotion, rejection, and rollback are durable and versioned.
- **Operational:** PufferLib is workload inside a governed worker, with leakage
  fences, reproducible seeds, compute budgets, interruption recovery, and no
  execution of bets.
- **Verdicts:** PRODUCT exposes candidate-versus-baseline judgment; EVIDENCE
  proves non-leaking held-out results; ONTOLOGY proves lineage and promotion;
  ATLAS checks training and promotion paths; CONSUMER proves comparison clarity;
  OPERATIONS proves budget/retry/rollback; FOUNDER alone promotes.

### R23 - Improve the Institution

- **User Value:** repeated failures produce inspectable improvement candidates
  instead of invisible prompt drift.
- **Ontology:** trace clusters, eval cases, prompt/playbook/harness/route
  candidates, Evaluations, and promotion/rollback are durable Artifacts and
  links. No model-weight change is allowed.
- **Operational:** held-out sets and evaluator independence are fenced; rejected
  candidates remain reproducible.
- **Verdicts:** PRODUCT shows one measurable workflow improvement; EVIDENCE
  binds source traces and holdout; ONTOLOGY proves candidate lineage; ATLAS
  checks proposal/promotion paths; CONSUMER proves behavior improved rather than
  merely metrics; OPERATIONS proves rollback; FOUNDER controls promotion.

### R24 - Own Intelligence

- **User Value:** Ryan can choose an owned, bounded football model when it beats
  the certified baseline on quality, cost, latency, and reliability.
- **Ontology:** training Dataset, Training Recipe Artifact, remote Compute
  Environment, Training Run, checkpoint or LoRA Artifact, held-out Golden
  Mission Evaluation, AgentDefinition/runtime candidate, promotion/rejection,
  serving revision, and rollback are one lineage.
- **Operational:** one exact open-weight model and one remote training route are
  admitted only after a compatibility probe; secrets remain outside agents;
  interrupted training and serving rollback are explicit.
- **Verdicts:** PRODUCT proves one served owned-model turn; EVIDENCE proves exact
  data/recipe/checkpoint/holdout; ONTOLOGY proves the lifecycle; ATLAS checks
  compute and serving seams; CONSUMER proves the model is a normal Dock
  participant; OPERATIONS proves budget, deployment, and rollback; FOUNDER alone
  promotes. A general training platform is out of scope.

### R25 - Deliver & Operate

- **User Value:** Ryan can reliably install and operate the founder product on
  Windows, recover his world, and repeat the first Mission.
- **Ontology:** backup and restore preserve the canonical Kernel and content
  hashes; installation or update metadata never becomes a truth store.
- **Operational:** current installer, supported upgrade, backup/restore,
  credential onboarding without agent access, health checks, clean shutdown,
  reproducible update, and rollback have a founder runbook.
- **Verdicts:** PRODUCT proves one installed Mission; EVIDENCE binds build and
  data receipts; ONTOLOGY proves restore equivalence; ATLAS checks shipped
  wiring; CONSUMER proves onboarding; OPERATIONS proves update/recovery/rollback;
  FOUNDER accepts delivery. Public SaaS, multi-user tenancy, a marketplace, and
  commercial launch are separate future decisions.

## R18 founder contract

R18 uses one bounded component of Ryan's broader **NFL Pressure Cascade** idea:

`NFL Pressure Cascade - QB Interception Component v0.1`

It screens the `player_pass_interceptions` market for one upcoming NFL event.
It is a research Technique, not a promise of true edge and not a bet placer.
Historical features use official nflverse play-by-play fields and a pinned raw
release; current market evidence uses The Odds API V4 for one event. The desired
bookmaker is Bovada. Exact Bovada/market availability is a runtime feasibility
fact, not a planning assumption.

The active R18 order must therefore begin with a no-write coverage door. If the
exact current market is unavailable, it stops and offers one bounded founder
choice: authorize one named alternate bookmaker from the same provider or
postpone/change the component. It may never substitute moneyline, a different
prop, or a different bookmaker silently.

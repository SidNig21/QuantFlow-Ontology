## In plain terms

Right now the part of the system that describes betting markets is written in the language of one
sport — competitors, events, odds. This order rewrites it in language that fits *any* market, so a
UFC fight and a crypto contract are the same four things wearing different data. It also teaches
the system that a betting slip can arrive from a human who already placed it, not only from a
strategy that proposed one.

**If it goes wrong:** the system quietly becomes a sports-betting database with generic labels on
it. Nothing breaks. You find out months later, when adding a second market means rewriting
everything above it.

## Founder direction binding this order (doctrine A7, stated 2026-07-25)

Sports betting on Bovada is the **first and only** market through the loop. Crypto/HyperLiquid is
explicitly later. **This does not relax the abstraction requirement** — it changes *when* the
abstraction gets tested, not *whether*. See "Why the market-agnostic shape still matters" below.

## The source of truth for this modelling — real Bovada slips

The founder supplied four real settled slips on 2026-07-25. **They are not in this repo and must
not be** — the repo is public and slips carry reference numbers and amounts. What follows is the
de-identified structure, which is all the schema needs. A synthetic example stands in:

```
Venue: Bovada
Ticket "2 Team Parlay", graded WIN, placed <timestamp>, external ref <opaque string>
  RISK <amount> · ODDS +390 · WINNINGS <amount>
  Leg 1  bout "Fighter A vs Fighter B" (starts <timestamp>)
         selection "Fighter A Wins by KO, TKO or DQ"   market type "(Bout) Method of Victory"
         price at selection -165        leg outcome WON
  Leg 2  bout "Fighter C vs Fighter D" (starts <timestamp>)
         selection "Fighter D Wins Inside Distance"    market type "(Bout) Alternate Method of Victory"
         price at selection +205        leg outcome WON
```

Four structural facts measured from the real slips, each load-bearing below:

1. **The same bout carries many distinct selections.** Two slips bet the same fight, placed the
   same minute, on different selections at different prices under different market types. So the
   bout and the betable selection are **different objects** — the bout is the occurrence, the
   selection is the instrument.
2. **Market type is a property, never a type.** Three seen in four slips: *(Bout) Method of
   Victory*, *(Bout) Alternate Method of Victory*, *(Bout) Fight Winner*. A type per market
   category is the Silo anti-pattern; these are values.
3. **A leg carries its own price and its own outcome.** One slip contains a **void** leg inside a
   losing ticket; another contains a leg with **no outcome at all** — never graded, because the
   parlay was already dead. A leg's state space is *not* the ticket's state space.
4. **A slip has an external identity** — the sportsbook's own reference string — which is what
   makes re-importing the same slip idempotent rather than duplicating it.

## Deliverables

### 1 · Rename the market plane to market-agnostic vocabulary

| Today | Becomes | What it is, from the slips |
|---|---|---|
| `event` | **`market_event`** | The bounded occurrence a market resolves against — a bout, a game, a settlement or funding event. **Keeps its state machine and all three commands.** |
| `market` | **`instrument`** | The betable/tradeable thing: one selection under one market type, or one perpetual contract. The market-agnostic pivot. |
| `odds_series` | **`quote`** | One priced observation at a timestamp: American odds, or bid/ask. |
| — | **`venue`** (new) | Where instruments are listed and priced: a sportsbook, an exchange. **Bovada has no home today.** |
| `competitor` | `competitor` (kept) | A participant in a contest. Justify retention in your report — see below. |
| `result` | `result` (kept) | Settled truth for a `market_event`. Justify retention in your report. |

Links rename with their endpoints: `offered_on` and `quotes` must read correctly against the new
names, and `venue` needs a `lists` link (`venue → instrument`). Keep link count changes minimal
and state the delta.

**`venue` is the one new object type this order may add, and it is not an exception to WO-108's
zero-new-types gate** — that gate governs *adding a second market*, and this is the first.

**On `competitor` and `result`:** the doctrine's charter names four market types and neither of
these is among them. They are kept because a bout genuinely has participants and a settled
outcome, and deleting them would lose information the slips contain. **But you must argue it in
one paragraph.** If your argument is that either is sport-specific and should go, implement your
argument — that is a legitimate outcome of this deliverable, not a deviation from it.

### 2 · Market-plane descriptions to the WO-101 register

Every object and property description in `market.ts` rewritten to: **first sentence what it is;
second the rule that governs it.** WO-101 deliberately left these alone so they would be written
once. `run`'s live description — ending *"Never clone types per pipeline step"* — is the bar. A

### 3 · `pipelineFed` — build the mechanism or drop the word

`pipelineFed` has **zero occurrences in the codebase.** It is doctrine vocabulary awaiting
machinery, and shipping the flag without an enforcer would repeat exactly the failure doctrine A5
names: *declaration is not capability.*

**Ruling: build it, with its enforcement, in this order.** A flag with no consumer is not
permitted; neither is dropping the Golden Hammer rule.

- Add `pipelineFed?: boolean` to the object definition surface.
- **`instrument` and `quote` are `pipelineFed: true`.** They are discovered and priced by a feed.
- **`venue`, `competitor`, `market_event` and `result` are not.** Note what this forces you to
  notice: `market_event` has three governed commands, so it *cannot* be pipeline-fed. That is the
  rule doing real work rather than decorating.
- **The enforcer, which must be able to go red:** a lint failing if any `pipelineFed: true` type
  is named by a creation command or by a transition command edge. The machinery exists —
  `lintCommands` already walks both lists.

WO-104 later reads the same flag to emit no write tools. This order gives it teeth today.

### 4 · The two `ticket` defects (doctrine A6)

**4a · The description points away from the founder's primary use case.** It currently reads *"one
proposed wager **emitted by a strategy**."* The founder's first use case is the inverse: a real
slip, already placed, supplied by a human, already graded by reality. An agent handed a real slip
and reading that description has been told this type is not for it. **Rewrite so it covers both
origins as equally primary.**

Add `origin` — an enum of `strategy_proposed | operator_supplied` — with a description that tells
an agent what each implies about trust and provenance. This is the field that makes the two
directions of doctrine A6 (forward product, backward calibration) distinguishable in data.

**4b · Fields the founder's real slips need and the type does not have.** All are property
additions to an `experimental` type, so the active-freeze lint does not object:

- `external_ref` — the venue's own ticket reference. The description must say it is the
  idempotency key for re-importing an operator-supplied slip, so the same slip twice is one row.
- `placed_at` — when the wager was struck. **Without it CLV cannot be computed**, which is the
  headline metric in the doctrine's evaluation loop. WO-101's verification already logged
  "nothing on the proof path carries a timestamp" as a finding; this is its first concrete cost.
- `payout` — realized return, distinct from `stake` and `combined_price`. Nullable while pending.

Fix `stake`'s description: it currently reads *"Simulated stake assigned by the sizing policy"*,
which is false for an operator-supplied slip carrying real money.

**4c · What you may NOT do here.** SCOPES routes the *creation* of an already-settled ticket to
**WO-103**, which owns creation. Do **not** add a transition edge, a command, or a creation path
for arrival-settled tickets. Adding a transition forces a `commands.ts` edge (`lintCommands`
rejects a legal transition with no command) and that is WO-103's call, not yours. Describe the
gap in your report; leave the machinery alone.

## Known findings — carry forward, do not fix here

Logged for **WO-103's author**. Recording them is in scope; acting on them is not.

1. **A parlay leg is a first-class fact the schema cannot express.** Measured from real slips: each
   leg carries its own price at selection *and* its own outcome, and one observed leg was `void`
   inside a losing ticket while another was never graded at all. `has_leg` is a property-less edge
   (`defineLink` takes only name, description, endpoints), so that data survives only inside the
   opaque `legs` JSON blob, which nothing can traverse. **This is the strongest known argument for
   link properties**, and it comes from the founder's own primary use case.
2. **`legs` and `has_leg` say the same thing twice** — a JSON array and a link both describing
   which markets a ticket bets. One of them is a second truth store. WO-103 decides which.
3. **A leg's state space is not the ticket's.** `ticket.grade` is `pending → win|loss|push|void`;
   observed legs additionally need "not graded." One enum is doing two incompatible jobs.
4. **Creation of an arrival-settled ticket** — deliverable 4c above. WO-103 owns it.
5. Findings 1–10 already logged in [`WO-101.md`](WO-101.md) stand unchanged.

If you find an eleventh, add it here rather than acting on it.

## Why the market-agnostic shape still matters (read before arguing with deliverable 1)

The founder has chosen sports-only through the loop, and that is binding. It is **not** a licence
to model Bovada directly. The reason is structural, not aspirational: you cannot tell a real
abstraction from a renamed Bovada schema by looking at Bovada. Both fit perfectly. The difference
only appears when something structurally unlike Bovada is pointed at the same types — which is
what G3 costs a day to find out, and what a rewrite would cost to find out later.

# WO-FOOTBALL-REPORT — Reproducible pregame football report

status: OPEN — founder approved this route on 2026-10-19 and replaced W2-02 as the active priority

## What this is

QuantFlow produces a reproducible pregame football report. The report carries QuantFlow's own forecast distribution, the evidence behind it, the assumptions, a market comparison, and later performance against actual results. Original means derived features, estimates, scenarios, and tested findings calculated from attributable source data. If the model finds no edge, the report shows that.

The route is [docs/SIMPLIFICATION_PLAN.md](../../SIMPLIFICATION_PLAN.md). W2-02 stays in the tree as parked work. It is not the build.

## Steps

1. Open the existing app and exercise its accepted research path. Repair the aged NFL capture check and the packaged-app readiness check without removing the protections they exercise.
2. For one player and one game, show historical usage, source times, coverage, and missing data. Identities must be right, inputs reproducible, and no future game may enter the pregame features.
3. One receiving-yards distribution for an upcoming player and game, with shared team pass opportunities and a recorded data version, method version, assumptions, and seed.
4. Score later historical weeks after the games, against a simple baseline, including calibration, error, and failure slices. Held-out weeks are the result. A file downloaded today is not proof of what was known before an old kickoff.
5. Put that research report on the existing Canvas. Every number traces to its calculation.

Betting tags, parlays, PufferLib, and Unsloth wait until a forecast has been tested. This order does not place a bet.

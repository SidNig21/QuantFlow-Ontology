## Register

product

## Users

QuantFlow is built first for one expert operator, Ryan, working on one Windows
Canvas. The ready Hermes Research Director is his primary colleague and default
coordinator, but Ryan may also address another governed participant or open a
capability directly. He watches distinct runtimes work from the same bounded
Kernel truth, steers them when needed, and inspects evidence, calculations, and
evaluation before acting outside the app.

## Product Purpose

QuantFlow is an ontology-centered quantitative research workspace. The Research
Director turns natural-language inquiries into internally scoped Kernel work,
recruits exact specialists, routes independent review, and returns evidence-led
research assessment plus, when a current Bovada expression exists, a separate
CANDIDATE/WATCH/PASS market judgment. The one Canvas makes the deliberately
opened work visible and steerable. Inspect reveals depth without becoming a
second world. The Kernel remains the sole source of truth. QuantFlow never
places a bet or trade.

The first complete institutional journey remains in flight, not present
behaviour. It uses real point-in-time UFC evidence to test a founder inquiry
without requiring a named Technique; NFL follows through the same architecture.
Recall and every learning loop remain downstream of independently evaluated,
source-timed work.

## Brand Personality

Precise, governed, alive. QuantFlow should feel like an expert instrument with
visible provenance and real activity, not a generic assistant or passive
dashboard. Palantir Foundry is a reference for ontology and trust; Databricks
agentic research is a reference for capable model-led work; Glacier is the
native QuantFlow visual language.

## Anti-references

- A chat wrapper over a terminal.
- Human controls and agent status detached in a side panel while the canvas is
  empty.
- Generic SaaS card grids, glass panels, finance navy-and-gold, or decorative
  neon.
- Machine-only green checks presented as proof that a visible feature works.
- Blocking onboarding screens between the operator and the desk.
- Hidden or flattened research lineage that requires reading logs.

## Design Principles

1. One ready Director is the default relationship; the Canvas is the only operating surface.
2. Put status and controls on the work, not in a detached observability panel.
3. Render only deliberately opened working surfaces; keep exact Kernel identity, history, and lineage available through Inspect.
4. Make uncertainty, refusal, and missing evidence visible before confidence.
5. Preserve expert density and familiar controls while keeping every claim
   attributable and falsifiable.
6. Cold open is clean except for the Director; close ends every owned process;
   durable work returns only when Ryan or the Director deliberately retrieves it.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Meet WCAG 2.1 AA contrast for text and state indicators. Normal text and terminal
input must work after explicit mouse focus, and focus may not become trapped.
Full global keyboard-navigation parity remains pre-release debt in
[`docs/DEBT.md` item 38](DEBT.md#L63). Meaning never depends on color alone. Motion
communicates state, respects reduced-motion preferences, and does not block the
operator's workflow.

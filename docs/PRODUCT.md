# Product

## Register

product

## Users

QuantFlow is built first for one expert operator, Ryan, working on a Windows
research desk. He states a bounded market question to one Hermes Research
Director, watches governed specialists perform the work, steers them when
needed, and inspects the evidence and evaluation before acting outside the app.

## Product Purpose

QuantFlow is an ontology-centered quantitative research and learning world. The
Research Director turns natural-language missions into durable Kernel objects,
recruits exact specialists, routes independent review, and returns cited
candidates or explicit no-candidate results. The canvas makes that work visible
and steerable. The Kernel remains the sole source of truth. QuantFlow never
places a bet or trade.

The first institutional journey grounds a founder-named Technique in real,
point-in-time football evidence. Recall and every learning loop remain
downstream of independently evaluated, source-timed work.

## Brand Personality

Precise, governed, alive. QuantFlow should feel like an expert instrument with
visible provenance and real activity, not a generic assistant or passive
dashboard. Palantir Foundry is a reference for ontology and trust; Databricks
agentic research is a reference for capable model-led work; Glacier is the
native QuantFlow visual language.

## Anti-references

- A chat wrapper over a terminal.
- Human controls and agent status detached in a side panel while the canvas is
  empty.
- Generic SaaS card grids, glass panels, finance navy-and-gold, or decorative
  neon.
- Machine-only green checks presented as proof that a visible feature works.
- Blocking onboarding screens between the operator and the desk.
- Hidden or flattened research lineage that requires reading logs.

## Design Principles

1. One Director is the front door; the canvas is the shared operating surface.
2. Put status and controls on the work, not in a detached observability panel.
3. Render Kernel objects, identities, and lineage as live inspectable entities.
4. Make uncertainty, refusal, and missing evidence visible before confidence.
5. Preserve expert density and familiar controls while keeping every claim
   attributable and falsifiable.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Meet WCAG 2.1 AA contrast for text and state indicators. Normal text and terminal
input must work after explicit mouse focus, and focus may not become trapped.
Full global keyboard-navigation parity remains pre-release debt in
[`docs/DEBT.md` item 38](DEBT.md#L63). Meaning never depends on color alone. Motion
communicates state, respects reduced-motion preferences, and does not block the
operator's workflow.

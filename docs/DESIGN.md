# Current visual contract

status: reference — visual/product register; not build or route authority

The existing Glacier Canvas remains the one product workspace. It never switches
into Mission, Focus, History, or Full Lineage worlds. A Mission is internal
Kernel context; history and lineage appear in the contextual Inspect drawer.

The Canvas contains only deliberately opened working surfaces: the ready
Director, recruited participants, capabilities Ryan or the Director opened, and
useful outputs selected for the desk. Kernel rows do not automatically become
tiles. A tile may summarize a bounded result without exposing raw ids, hashes,
paths, sessions, or every intermediate object; exact detail remains available
in Inspect.

The Dock is a collapsible governed catalog, not a mode switcher or second
workspace. Its classes are Participants, Data, Tools, Methods, and Compute.
Participant identity selects and inspects; only an explicitly labelled action
can stop a participant. Dock, Canvas, and Inspect consume the same derived facts.
Species and process detail are secondary to role, current Task, state, and output.

Participant tiles are working-sized and may expose their full TUI on demand.
Ordinary terminals are neutral bench equipment and never masquerade as governed
participants. Cables are absent by default; selection may show eligible
connections and active work may show the exact Task, capability, or Artifact
handoff. No connection means no cable, and the full Ontology graph is never the
normal Canvas.

The visible answer separates research assessment from market actionability.
Current authority is derived from Kernel Report lineage; prior revisions remain
retrievable through Inspect. Cold open shows only the Director, and closing the
application ends every QuantFlow-owned process. No new ontology, link kind, or
Canvas truth store is introduced by this presentation contract.
